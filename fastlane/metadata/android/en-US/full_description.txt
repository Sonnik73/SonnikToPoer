A strength training log with no internet, ads, trackers or sign-up. All data stays on your phone.

<b>Workouts</b>
<ul>
<li>Exercises with any mix of reps, weight, time, distance and bodyweight</li>
<li>Workout templates, plus a template built for your most rested muscles</li>
<li>Today's target: +1 rep over the last workout, and the next weight from your inventory at the top of the rep range</li>
<li>Rest timer that works with the screen locked</li>
<li>Inventory: dumbbells, kettlebells, resistance bands with a from–to range, weight vests</li>
<li>83 default exercises with how-to, including pull-up bar work</li>
</ul>

<b>Muscles</b>
<ul>
<li>Anatomical body map of 23 muscles colored from red to green by recovery</li>
<li>What each muscle does and every exercise for it</li>
<li>Live countdown to full recovery and a 7-day volume mode</li>
<li>Template readiness based on their muscles' recovery</li>
<li>Configurable notifications and a home screen widget</li>
</ul>

<b>Statistics</b>
<ul>
<li>Records, estimated 1RM, progress charts, volume, plateau hints</li>
<li>Smartwatch data entered manually: heart rate, duration, calories</li>
<li>A "Share results" card with your week</li>
<li>JSON backup, CSV export and a backup file on Google Drive via the Save to… dialog — no Google sign-in</li>
<li>Themes: light, dark, black background and seven palettes</li>
</ul>

Russian and English interface.

The GitHub version can also check for updates and keep dated backups with Google sign-in; the F-Droid version has neither and never uses the network.
