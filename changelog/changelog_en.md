# What's new in SonnikToPower

## 1.38
- Body measurements: a figure on the home screen with dashed lines — biceps, forearm, chest, waist — showing the latest values and change; Measure now saves girths in cm with the date, plus a history with a chart

## 1.37
- A more feminine female muscle map: slimmer waist, wider hips, narrower shoulders, fuller chest, long hair with a bun

## 1.36
- A female muscle map: the app asks once on first launch, and you can switch in Settings → Appearance — figure, proportions and chest shape change on the maps, the widget and the share card

## 1.35
- Links to GitHub in About: star the project and leave feedback, an idea or a bug report

## 1.34
- Reproducible build for F-Droid: the F-Droid version will carry the same signature as GitHub, so you can switch between them without reinstalling

## 1.33
- F-Droid preparation: a separate build without Google services and without network access; refreshed store description and screenshots

## 1.32
- What each muscle does: on the map, “What this muscle does” for the selected one (with an Exercises button listing all exercises for it) and “How the muscles work” — all muscles in one list with descriptions

## 1.31
- New muscles on the map: brachialis, brachioradialis, serratus anterior, teres major, infraspinatus (rotator cuff) and tibialis anterior; muscles of all default exercises reviewed (updated where you have not changed them)
- A more detailed, three-dimensional muscle map: light and shade, collarbones, sternum, spine
- 20 new exercises: on the pull-up bar and for the new muscles

## 1.30
- A more compact rest timer: one row with ±15 s, the time and Skip, plus a thin progress line — more room for your sets

## 1.29
- A backup file on Google Drive without Google sign-in, as in Pin: create it via the Save to… dialog and the app keeps it updated after workouts; on a new phone your data is restored from that file

## 1.28
- Update checks, as in Pin: on start (at most every 6 hours) and with a button in Settings; the new version downloads from GitHub
- Backups on Google Drive: automatically every 12 hours if data changed, and on demand; restore from a list of backups

## 1.27
- Background transparency for the Muscle status widget: a slider in Settings from solid to fully transparent, as in Pin

## 1.26
- Appearance settings: light, dark or system theme and seven color palettes — Steel, Forest, Sunset, Amethyst, Graphite and the themed Forge and Garrison; Wallpaper on Android 12+
- Black background in the dark theme for AMOLED screens

## 1.25
- Preparing to share data with the author's other apps

## 1.24
- Fixed: the keyboard no longer stays open on the workout screen after saving a set

## 1.23
- Statistics → By exercise is sorted by the number of workouts in the selected period, most frequent first

## 1.22
- Value labels on charts: numbers above points and bars; tap or drag to see the exact value and date
- Period selection in statistics: Week, Month, Year, All — for overall and per-exercise stats

## 1.21
- Filters in the exercise list: search by name or muscle, body area (chest, back, shoulders, arms, core, legs) and equipment; a found counter and Reset

## 1.20
- GPL-3.0 license: the app is now open source and being prepared for F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid
- Version code is now fixed (1.20 → 120) instead of the build number — updates install as before

## 1.19
- Swipe left and right to switch between Workout, History, Statistics, Exercises and Inventory; Back returns to Workout

## 1.18
- How-to for all 63 default exercises: setup, movement and the main mistake to avoid. Your exercises with the same names got it automatically
- The ⓘ icon next to an exercise in a workout shows the technique; edit it in the exercise dialog

## 1.17
- Report image, CSV and backup file names include date and time: SonnikToPower-2026-10-08_14-35.png

## 1.16
- “Share results” on the Statistics screen: an image with your week — workouts this week, in 30 days and in total, weeks in a row, volume and kcal, muscles trained this week and the week's records. Sent via the regular Share menu to any messenger

## 1.15
- More detailed muscle map: fibers follow each muscle's shape (fanning on the chest, delts, lats and glutes, arcing along the limbs), veins on the arms, lower abdomen and calves, light tendons — kneecap, Achilles, elbow and back of the knee

## 1.14
- “Build a workout for rested muscles” button in templates: creates a template of 4–6 exercises for the longest-rested muscles, skipping exercises that load muscles still recovering; familiar exercises are preferred, compound ones come first. The template opens for editing right away

## 1.13
- 50 new exercises — at least three for every muscle group — with your equipment: dumbbells, bands, spring expander, weight vest, pull-up bar, bench. Each has main muscles and a rep range; exercises with matching names are not duplicated

## 1.12
- Readiness color on templates: a stripe from red (the workout's muscles are still recovering) to green (all recovered) and “Readiness N%”
- Templates are sorted by readiness — the best fit for today comes first

## 1.11
- New anatomical body map: shaped muscles, ab blocks, heads of the quads, hamstrings and calves, subtle volume and fibers
- Larger map on the home screen; tap a muscle to see its name and time to recovery (or weekly volume)
- The widget and the “Muscle names” guide use the new map

## 1.10
- “Muscle status” home screen widget: body map in recovery colors and time to full recovery; tap to open the app
- Export all sets to CSV for Excel and Google Sheets (Settings → Backup)

## 1.9
- “Volume, 7 days” mode on the muscle map: working sets per muscle and which muscles lack volume (target: 10–20 a week)
- Templates on the home screen show whether their muscles are ready; ready templates come first
- Notifications (Settings → Notifications): when all or selected muscles have recovered, or daily at a set time; a reminder after a break in training; quiet hours

## 1.8
- Today's target under each exercise: +1 rep over the last workout, and the next weight from your inventory at the top of the rep range
- A new set is prefilled with the target — just tap Save
- Rep range per exercise (e.g. 8–12 or 20–40) in the exercise dialog
- Estimated one-rep max (1RM) next to the record

## 1.7
- “Muscle names” guide: the ⓘ button next to the body map and in the exercise dialog — tap a muscle on the figure or a name to see where it is

## 1.6
- Smooth muscle color transition from red to green as it recovers, gradient legend
- Live countdown to recovery of each muscle (h:mm:ss) and to full recovery of all muscles

## 1.5
- Muscle status on the home screen: body front and back, red (recovering) to green (ready), gray — not trained for a long time
- “How hard was it?” after the last set of an exercise sets the recovery time (24–96 h); several exercises for one muscle extend it a bit
- Main muscles for exercises with a suggestion by name; existing exercises got their muscles automatically
- Default exercise set for a fresh install

- Refreshed calm interface: consistent colors instead of wallpaper colors, fewer borders and fills, tidier cards
- Clear version numbers: 1.4, 1.5… instead of build numbers

- Entry dialog when finishing a workout, editable later on the workout screen
- Watch summary in history, totals in statistics
